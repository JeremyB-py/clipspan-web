Features

Features

ClipSpan keeps clipboard history on your devices. Sync supported clips automatically or send one item when you choose.

  • History

    On-device clipboard history

    Recent clips stay searchable on the device that captured them. Removed items can be restored from Recently removed.

  • Send

    Send Clip between paired devices

    Choose when an item should move instead of syncing every copy automatically. Share sheet and selected text use the same Send Clip action.

  • Pairing

    Local network pairing

    Pair over your Wi-Fi with a QR code, without an account, or type the pairing link if the phone has no camera. Wi-Fi sync is encrypted, and the phone checks the computer's key from the QR code. Local-only mode never contacts ClipSpan servers.

  • Offline

    Catches up after time offline

    Clips copied while a device was offline reach your other devices when it reconnects, in the order you copied them, without replacing what's on their clipboard.

  • Android

    One Android app, including the keyboard

    History, pairing, and ClipSpan Keyboard ship in a single install. Enable the keyboard in Android settings to paste into any app. Sync stays off in password fields.

  • Media

    Images and other multimedia

    Text and images sync between Android, Linux, and Windows. Files and links are in active testing. Automatic sync still respects your size and sensitive-content settings.

  • Account

    Optional account, backup, and relay

    Sign in to ClipSpan Cloud for encrypted history backup and relay of text and images when you're away from your Wi-Fi. Your password never leaves your device. Same-account devices on the same Wi-Fi can pair without a QR scan. Self-host with a custom Account API URL.

Privacy

How clipboard data is handled

ClipSpan defaults to local use and makes cloud sync optional. See the privacy policy.

  • Yes

    Is clipboard data end-to-end encrypted?

    Yes, on both paths. Wi-Fi sync runs device to device over TLS, and the phone checks the computer's key from the QR code. Through ClipSpan Cloud, clips and images are end-to-end encrypted, and the servers never receive clipboard plaintext.

  • Yes

    Can it work without a cloud service?

    Yes. QR pairing on the LAN works with no account. An account uses ClipSpan Cloud. Self-host with a custom Account API URL.

  • Yes

    Is history stored on-device?

    Yes. History lives on your devices. Cloud history backup, when used, is encrypted under keys your clients hold.

Defaults and choices

  • Synchronization is optional. Send Clip moves an item without turning on automatic sync.
  • Copies a password manager marks as sensitive stay out of History and never sync, unless you change that in Settings.
  • Password fields on Android keep sync actions disabled, and ClipSpan Keyboard pauses sync in private browser tabs.
  • ClipSpan Keyboard can see text in the focused field, like any Android keyboard. Keystrokes stay on the device unless you copy or send a clip.
  • You choose whether to enable account sync at all. Encrypted history backup is optional, and your account password never leaves your device.
  • Diagnostics and bug reports omit clipboard contents, tokens, and pairing secrets.
  • An account uses ClipSpan Cloud (api.clipspan.com), which relays text and images when you're away from your Wi-Fi. You can self-host with a custom Account API URL instead.
  • Local QR pairing stays the default if you never sign in. A phone paired by typing the short link, instead of scanning the QR code, trusts the first computer it reaches.
  • Delete your account from the app, or on the delete account page.
