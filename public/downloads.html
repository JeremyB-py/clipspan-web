Linux

64-bit (x86_64)

Your device

Debian or Ubuntu: install with apt Recommended

Adds the ClipSpan repository, so updates arrive with your other system updates. Put your tester code in place of YOUR_CODE. 

curl -fsSL -u "clipspan:YOUR_CODE" https://apt.clipspan.com/install.sh | sudo bash
AppImage Any distribution. Make it executable, then run it. It updates itself. .deb package Debian or Ubuntu without the repository. No automatic updates.

Android

Phones and tablets, through Google Play's testing program

Your device

  1. Join the test while signed in to the Google account you signed up with.

    Join the test play.google.com/apps/testing/com.clipspan.app

  2. Then install ClipSpan from Google Play. Updates arrive through Google Play too.

    Install from Google Play play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.clipspan.app

If Google Play says you can't join, or you signed up with an address that isn't a Google account, email contact@clipspan.com with the Google account you use on your phone and we'll add it. If you installed a ClipSpan APK before, uninstall it first: the Google Play version is signed differently.
