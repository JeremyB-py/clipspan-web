Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

Your device
Download the installer

Your browser or antivirus may block ClipSpan by mistake

ClipSpan is new, and security software is wary of files few people have downloaded yet. It may warn about the installer or remove it even though nothing is wrong with it. These warnings should stop as more people install ClipSpan.

  • Your browser says the file isn't commonly downloaded: choose Keep. In Edge, open the download's … menu, choose Keep, then Show more and Keep anyway.
  • Windows protected your PC: choose More info, then Run anyway.
  • The file disappeared or was quarantined: restore it. In Windows Security, open Virus & threat protection, then Protection history, select the ClipSpan entry, and choose Actions, then Restore. Other antivirus apps have a similar quarantine list.

The installer is code-signed, and Windows shows the publisher as Jeremy Brown. If it shows anyone else, don't run it. If your antivirus flags ClipSpan, tell us which one at contact@clipspan.com so we can report the mistake. After the first install, ClipSpan updates itself.

For managed or scripted installs, there's also an MSI package.

Android

Phones and tablets, through Google Play's testing program

Your device

  1. Sign up to test with the email address of the Google account on your phone. Google Play's test is invite-only, so we add that account to it and email you when it's done.

  2. Join the test while signed in to that Google account.

    Join the test play.google.com/apps/testing/com.clipspan.app

  3. Then install ClipSpan from Google Play. Updates arrive through Google Play too.

    Install from Google Play play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.clipspan.app

If Google Play says you can't join, we may not have added you yet, or you signed up with an address that isn't a Google account. Email contact@clipspan.com with the Google account you use on your phone and we'll add it. If you installed a ClipSpan APK before, uninstall it first: the Google Play version is signed differently.
