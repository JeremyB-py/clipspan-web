Pairing

Pair with ClipSpan

Open ClipSpan on your phone, tap Scan QR code, and scan the code on your computer again.

Your phone's camera can read the code, but only the ClipSpan app can finish pairing.

Using an older ClipSpan?

In ClipSpan, tap Can't scan? Enter the pairing link and paste this link:

The link stops working after a few minutes. Don't share it. You can also update ClipSpan from Google Play, then scan the code again.

Don't have ClipSpan yet?

ClipSpan for Android is in testing on Google Play.

  1. Join the test while signed in to the Google account you signed up with.

    Join the test play.google.com/apps/testing/com.clipspan.app

  2. Then install ClipSpan from Google Play.

    Install from Google Play play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.clipspan.app

  3. Open ClipSpan, tap Scan QR code, and scan the code on your computer again. The code you scanned with the camera doesn't carry over to the new app.

If Google Play says you can't join, email contact@clipspan.com with the Google account you use on your phone and we'll add it. Not testing yet? Join testing.
