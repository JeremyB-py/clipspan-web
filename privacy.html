Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026 · Applies to clipspan.com and the ClipSpan apps

ClipSpan is a clipboard history and synchronization product for Android, Linux, and Windows. This page covers the public website, early-access signup, and the apps that testers will install.

What this site collects

If you use the early-access form, we collect:

  • Your email address
  • Platform preferences you select
  • Whether you are interested in testing an early build
  • Any optional notes you write
  • Basic technical metadata from the form provider (such as submission time)

We use this information only to contact you about ClipSpan development, early testing, and related product updates you requested.

What we do not do with signup data

  • We do not sell your email address
  • We do not share it with advertisers
  • We do not ask you to create a ClipSpan account just to join the waitlist

Form delivery

Form submissions are processed by a third-party form provider configured for this site (for example Formspree). That provider processes the submission in order to deliver it to us. Review their privacy terms when the provider is connected.

Analytics

If privacy-conscious analytics or server logs are enabled later, they will be used to understand aggregate traffic (for example, whether a referral source sent visitors). They should not be used to build advertising profiles. This policy will be updated when analytics are added.

Product data principles

On Android, ClipSpan is one app: the companion UI and ClipSpan Keyboard install together. Local-only remains the default. An account is optional.

  • Clipboard history lives on each device. Pairing on your LAN sends clips between devices you accept.
  • If you sign in, the account server stores your email, device names and platforms, and encrypted vault or relay payloads. Servers do not receive clipboard plaintext.
  • Encrypted history backup is optional. Vault contents are encrypted on the device before upload.
  • ClipSpan does not sell clipboard contents, keystrokes, or account data. There is no advertising SDK in the apps.

ClipSpan Keyboard

Android shows a system warning when you enable a keyboard because an input method can see text in the focused field. That is how any third-party keyboard works.

ClipSpan Keyboard uses that access to type, to offer history paste, and to record clips you copy or send. It does not send what you type to ClipSpan servers. Keystrokes stay on the device unless you copy them or send a clip, and then sync that clip to a paired device or an encrypted vault you chose to use. You can turn the keyboard off in Android Settings at any time.

Clipboard, pairing, and diagnostics

  • History, pins, hidden items, and Recently removed are stored on the device.
  • LAN sync uses a pairing secret you scan or enter. Do not share pairing QR codes or tokens.
  • Automatic sync can include text and images you copy, subject to your size and sensitive-content settings.
  • Diagnostics and bug reports omit clipboard contents, tokens, pairing secrets, and passwords.

Self-hosted account and relay operators can see ciphertext and routing metadata on disk. They cannot read vault or relay contents without your password, recovery key, or device keys.

Contact and deletion

Email contact@clipspan.com to ask questions, request removal from the early-access list, or correct your address.

Children

ClipSpan is not directed at children under 13. Do not create an account for a child under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site and product mature. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
