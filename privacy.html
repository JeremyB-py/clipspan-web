Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026 · Applies to clipspan.com

ClipSpan is a clipboard history and synchronization product currently in development. This page covers the public website and early-access signup. Product privacy for the apps will be expanded as public builds ship.

What this site collects

If you use the early-access form, we collect:

  • Your email address
  • Platform preferences you select
  • Whether you are interested in testing an early build
  • Any optional notes you write
  • Basic technical metadata from the form provider (such as submission time)

We use this information only to contact you about ClipSpan development, early testing, and related product updates you requested.

What we do not do with signup data

  • We do not sell your email address
  • We do not share it with advertisers
  • We do not ask you to create a ClipSpan account just to join the waitlist

Form delivery

Form submissions are processed by a third-party form provider configured for this site (for example Formspree). That provider processes the submission in order to deliver it to us. Review their privacy terms when the provider is connected.

Analytics

If privacy-conscious analytics or server logs are enabled later, they will be used to understand aggregate traffic (for example, whether a referral source sent visitors). They should not be used to build advertising profiles. This policy will be updated when analytics are added.

Product data principles

For the ClipSpan apps themselves:

  • Local-only mode can run without contacting ClipSpan account or relay servers.
  • Optional account/relay sync is designed so servers store ciphertext, not clipboard plaintext.
  • Clipboard history is stored on your devices. Encrypted cloud backup is optional.

More detailed in-app disclosures will ship with public builds and may be linked from this page later.

Contact and deletion

Email contact@clipspan.com to ask questions, request removal from the early-access list, or correct your address.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site and product mature. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
